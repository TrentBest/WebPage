01 / What is a Digiten?

A Digiten is a persistent inhabitant of the digital world — with an identity, a place in that world, and behavior expressed through the technology that composes its experience.

A sprite is a representation. An FSM is a behavior mechanism. A neural network is a possible decision mechanism. A Digiten is the citizen those mechanisms can help bring to life. Its visible avatar is one representation of an entity whose identity and world state need not be tied to a single renderer.

The canonical Digiten Architecture explainer defines the intended model in more depth: identity, goals, role, persistent state, policy, and world capabilities.

This page is an architectural explainer. It does not claim that every described capability is already implemented or that this illustration is a live population simulation.

02 / A citizen, not a rendering trick

Identity & persistence

Who the Digiten is, where it belongs, and which durable facts should survive a disconnect or a change of view.

Behavior is composed

FSMs and other decision mechanisms may participate in behavior. The Digiten itself is not synonymous with any one mechanism.

Many representations

The same citizen may appear in a floor plan, a close-up scene, a city view, or another renderer without becoming a different citizen.

03 / Technology to scale

From one citizen to Singularity City

The Workshop is intended to let people compose, inspect, modify, and publish digital-world experiences. An Experience is an environment comprised of MicroBundles. Bundles are focused units of capability, behavior, content, and sensory contribution; providers and renderers must be grounded in the actual contracts that implement them. The goal is scale without requiring every experience to reinvent its own citizen model.

04 / People and Digitens share a world

The player is part of the population

A connected human player can be represented through an avatar in the same digital world as other Digitens. The current WebPage source explicitly describes civic officials as Digitens and player avatars as Digitens when connected. Presence and persistence are distinct: a live avatar may disappear when disconnected while durable world state remains.

Source-backed example: the current WebPage code contains a civic Digiten catalog with a mayor, councilors, clerk, treasury, planning, public works, housing, transit, and digital-services officials. That is a concrete catalog, not evidence that a massive city simulation is already running.

05 / Singularity City · an editable world

The Workshop stays at the heart of the city.

Singularity City is the first anchor of Singularity World: a super-massive floating city in the Pacific, with the Workshop fixed at its center. Districts, buildings, commerce, residences, transit, civic spaces, and portals can grow outward around that anchor. Digitens live, work, travel, serve, create, and visit within this world; casinos have a place in its economy instead of existing as disconnected demos.

Read the Singularity City Experience design for the current source-backed foundations, intended MicroBundle/provider boundaries, and acceptance gates.
